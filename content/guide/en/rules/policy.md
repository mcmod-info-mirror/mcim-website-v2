MCIM is a mirror service that exists to give users in mainland China stable access to mod information. The terms below protect the rights of mod authors and of the upstream platforms.

This is a translation. Where it differs from the Chinese original, the Chinese text is authoritative.

## File ownership

Ownership of every file MCIM mirrors, apart from MCIM's own configuration, follows the terms of the upstream platform it came from. Redistributing or republishing anything through MCIM in any form without permission from the original copyright holder is forbidden.

## Disclaimer

MCIM makes a best effort to keep the mirrored information complete, valid and current. MCIM accepts no legal liability for any dispute or obligation arising from its use; you use it at your own risk.

## No wrapper services

Wrapping MCIM's endpoints in another service is forbidden.

MCIM reserves the right to take whatever measures are necessary, up to terminating service, in response to any breach of the above.

## Other

- This is a non-profit public service. Please do not attack it.
- API downloads do not count towards mod author revenue. Downloading from the official source or from the mirror earns the author nothing either way, and complaints on that basis will not be entertained.
